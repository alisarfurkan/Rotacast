Source: Prof. Dr. Fetullah Arık (KTÜN), statements of August 2026 (NTV, GZT, Milliyet)
Sinkholes are the face of this loss on the ground. According to Prof. Dr. Fetullah Arık (August 2026), more than 740 sinkholes have been recorded in the Konya Closed Basin; the Ministry of Agriculture’s 2023 drought plan gave 610. Around Karapınar about 500 were counted in 2020; today it is about 600. More than 40 new sinkholes formed in the last year alone. As groundwater is pumped out, the roofs of limestone cavities lose their support and collapse.
