On this Çumra field the Balanced transition card cuts irrigation water from 4,829 to 1,340 m³ per hectare: 72% less water. Estimated gross income on the crop-sequence part rises by 55%; this excludes costs and the income of the cumin share, because cumin has no verified farm-gate price. We have two rules: no option uses more irrigation water or causes more erosion than today, and only crops documented in that zone are used.
