In Çumra NASA POWER shows 533 mm of rain a year; CHIRPS, satellite rain blended with stations, shows 302 mm. Rotacast rescales POWER by this ratio every month.

Also: CHIRPS rain (UCSB), SoilGrids soil (ISRIC), MGM, TurkStat, Konya agriculture office
For every field the farmer picks, temperature, rain, humidity, wind, solar radiation, slope, elevation and vegetation come from six NASA data products. We also correct NASA data with ground-based sources: NASA POWER shows 533 mm of rain a year in Çumra, while CHIRPS, satellite rain blended with station observations, shows 302 mm; we rescale POWER rain every month by the ratio of the two. GPM IMERG is kept as a backup rain source. GRACE is not used as field data but as the basin’s water trend.
