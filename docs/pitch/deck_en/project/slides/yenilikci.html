Rotacast does not only suggest traditional crops. Using Konya’s 7 climate zones, it also lists innovative crops documented by official sources in each zone: rain-fed spices like cumin and fenugreek, sorghum as an alternative to maize, and perennial lavender. We compute their water needs with coefficients from peer-reviewed literature. But we never invent income for a crop whose price we could not verify; the card says “not computed”.
