Source: UNESCO World Heritage List, Neolithic Site of Çatalhöyük (2012)
…and focused on the future of Konya, a land with 9,000 years of farming. Çatalhöyük, one of the oldest known farming settlements in the world, lies in Çumra, a few kilometres from our demo field, and has been a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2012. Konya was the Seljuk capital of Anatolia and is today Türkiye’s largest province and its breadbasket. But the water under this heritage is running out: the same crops with the same water cannot go on.
