The same analysis comes in two views. In Beginner mode the farmer sees simple cards that compare each option with the current rotation: an arrow each for water, income and soil health, a short “Why?” and who to ask. In Advanced mode the agronomist sees the whole card: score breakdown, year-by-year planting calendar, irrigation water range, income, yield, nitrogen balance, erosion, frost and heat risk.
