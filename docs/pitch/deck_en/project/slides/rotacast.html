Built to ease farming’s pressure on water

Rotacast reads 25 years of the field’s satellite memory: when it turned green each year and when it lay bare.

It explains the result in plain words; the farmer and the agronomist make the decision.

Beginner: simple cards

Advanced: soil, irrigation method, water quota

Rotacast · open source (MIT)
Rotacast cannot stop sinkholes on its own, but it was built to ease farming’s pressure on groundwater. We built it only for Konya, around Konya’s problems. A farmer picks the field on the map and tells what they grow today, and Rotacast tells them about their field. A farmer sees simple cards in Beginner mode; an agronomist can enter a soil test, the irrigation method and a water quota in Advanced mode.
